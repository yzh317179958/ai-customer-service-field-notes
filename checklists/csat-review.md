# How to review customer satisfaction ratings

One overall CSAT number hides what is going on. This review takes the ratings from a few days and finds what to fix. Background and a worked example with 217 ratings: [CSAT for AI customer service: what low scores meant](https://ai.dingboai.com/en/csat-ai-customer-service.html) and [What is CSAT?](https://ai.dingboai.com/en/what-is-csat.html).

## Collect

- [ ] Every rating from a few days. Not a sample.
- [ ] For each rating: the reply it followed.
- [ ] For each rating: who sent that reply, AI or a person.
- [ ] For each rating: how long after the reply the survey went out.

## Group

- [ ] Replies the AI sent alone.
- [ ] Tickets a person closed, surveyed at closing.
- [ ] Tickets surveyed after the customer went silent.
- [ ] Messages the company sent before the customer had written.
- [ ] Count the share of low ratings (1 and 2 out of 5) in each group.

## Read every low rating

Sort each one into a cause:

- [ ] wrong fact
- [ ] unfinished action: the customer is still waiting for a part, a refund or a follow-up
- [ ] delay
- [ ] wrong language
- [ ] wording
- [ ] survey timing

## Fix

- [ ] Fix the largest group first.
- [ ] Count again a week later.

## Survey timing rules

- [ ] Send the survey when the matter is finished, not when the reply is sent.
- [ ] If the reply promised something a colleague still has to do, wait until it can have arrived.
- [ ] Do not survey a customer about a message they did not ask for.
- [ ] One survey per matter.

## Beyond the survey

- [ ] For every reply, rated or not, look at what the customer did next: wrote again with the same question, or did not.
